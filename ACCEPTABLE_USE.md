# Acceptable Use

NearView is a private collaboration tool for screen sharing across a trusted LAN or virtual private network.

The project does not provide:

- public room discovery;
- anonymous public chat or voice rooms;
- internet relay, TURN service, or built-in VPN transport;
- recording, covert capture, or remote control.

Users must not use NearView to:

- view another person's screen without their knowledge and consent;
- harass, threaten, stalk, defraud, or impersonate others;
- steal credentials, private data, or intellectual property;
- distribute illegal content or facilitate other unlawful activity;
- bypass organizational access controls or monitoring rules.

The host of a room can read room text because it is relayed through the host's signaling process. Screen and system-audio media use WebRTC encryption between peers, but a compromised endpoint or malicious host can still capture what it receives.

This document is a product boundary, not legal advice. Before operating a public service, collecting reports, offering public rooms, or expanding into voice and messaging, the distributor should obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review and implement abuse reporting, retention, moderation, and law-enforcement response procedures.
